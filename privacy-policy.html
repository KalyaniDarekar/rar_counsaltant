Clause 01 · Overview

Introduction & Regulatory Scope

This Privacy Policy & Data Protection Notice is framed in compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act), the Information Technology Act, 2000, and the IT (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011.

Under the DPDP Act, RAR Consultant acts as a Data Fiduciary regarding personal information of clients, partners, and visitors. This notice governs digital personal data collected within India (or digitized later), and processing outside India connected to offering goods or services to Data Principals within India.

Clause 03 · Section 6 DPDP Act

Purpose of Processing & Lawful Grounds

1. Informed consent: freely given, specific, informed, unconditional, and unambiguous, through affirmative action when submitting request forms.

2. Legitimate uses (Sec 7): fulfilling contractual duties, generating quotes you requested, or responding to legal mandates.

You receive notice in clear, plain language before consent is collected, covering the data, purpose, and how to exercise rights or complain.

Clause 04 · Email Dispatch Process

Data Processor Architecture

Your web inquiry flows: Secure form submission (HTTPS/SSL) → secure PHPMailer direct email relay → official inbox anand@rarconsultant.co.in.

  • Direct email delivery: submissions go to our corporate mailbox, never to public third-party databases.
  • Direct processing: inquiries are reviewed solely by our team.
  • Strict confidentiality: your details are never rented, shared, or sold to advertisers or marketing agencies.
Clause 05 · Data Security & Storage

Cross-Border Transmission & Safeguards

Under Section 16 of the DPDP Act, the Central Government may restrict transfer of personal data to specified foreign jurisdictions. We enforce these boundaries and verify that external cloud relays comply, and will not route data into any territory blacklisted by MeitY.

All correspondence is protected by industry-standard SSL/TLS encryption, with strict access controls.

Clause 06 · Chapter III DPDP Act

Data Principal Rights

  • Right to Access (Sec 11): a summary of data processed, activities conducted, and others it has been shared with.
  • Right to Correction (Sec 12): correction of inaccurate data, completion of incomplete records, erasure of data no longer needed.
  • Right of Grievance (Sec 13): readily available grievance redressal.
  • Right to Nominate (Sec 14): nominate someone to exercise your rights in case of death or incapacity.

Clause 09 · DPBI Adjudication

Escalation to the Data Protection Board of India

Under Sections 18 and 27, if you are aggrieved by a Data Fiduciary's order on a grievance or receive no determination in reasonable time, you may file an electronic complaint with the Data Protection Board of India (DPBI), established by the Central Government to receive intimations, direct urgent remedial measures, inquire into breaches, and adjudicate penalties.

You may use the DPBI portal once operational, or lodge formal written notices as prescribed.
